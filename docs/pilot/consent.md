# Pilot consent (draft for departmental review)
Status: draft, revised 2026-10-31 (first draft 2026-10-24). **Not to be used until the
department and the guide approve it.** It describes what the software does; it is not
legal advice and makes no claim of legal compliance beyond the review it awaits. The
design follows the principles of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023
(NFR-3, ADR-0044); whether it meets them is for the reviewers to judge.

Points for the reviewers are marked **[review]**.

## What Ripple is
A research prototype built for a final-year B.Tech project. It helps you track
internship and job openings. It remembers facts you give it (CGPA, batch, skills,
projects, preferences), openings you paste or forward, and your applications, and it
updates its own conclusions when those facts change. It never applies anywhere, sends
nothing to recruiters and never acts on any website for you.

## Taking part is voluntary
Taking part, or leaving, has no effect on your placement process, grades or standing
with the department. **[review]** Confirm who, other than the author, may know who
takes part.

## What we collect
- What you type, paste, forward or upload (resume text), and the messages you send the
  bot.
- Openings the Scout finds from public sources you have enabled.
- What was sent to you and when (digests, alerts), and the buttons you press about a
  recommendation (saved, applied, not interested) or a question (useful, not useful),
  with an optional short note you may type.
- Optionally, a weekly survey (would you have found this opening yourself, and how
  much you trusted Ripple this week), answered outside the app under an anonymous code.
Not collected: contacts, browsing data, location, anything from other apps.

## How it is used
To rank openings, remind you of deadlines, ask you when a fact may have changed, and
measure whether the system keeps its memory correct and useful. "Applied" means only
that you told Ripple you applied; Ripple does not check or report it to anyone.
Research results report aggregates only (for example, the share of shown openings
that students saved); no student is named or identifiable in any report, thesis or
paper, and a week with fewer than five active students is kept out of the published
results. Your notes and messages are never quoted without your separate written
permission.

## Where it lives and who sees it
On one server operated by the author, with encrypted backups kept for 30 days.
**[review]** Server location and provider to be confirmed before sign-off. Model calls
send the relevant memory text to a third-party LLM provider; see the provider's data
policy linked in the dashboard. Only the author has access to the database; the guide
sees aggregate reports only.

## Your rights
- See everything the system believes about you and why (dashboard, "Memories").
- Correct or delete any single memory, or everything (Account → Delete everything).
  Deleting a memory also deletes the buttons you pressed and notes you typed about it;
  deleting everything removes your memories, uploads, delivery records and feedback at
  once. Backups expire within 30 days.
- Export all your data as JSON at any time.
- Withdraw at any time by deleting your account or telling the author.
