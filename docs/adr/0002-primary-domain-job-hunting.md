# ADR-0002: Job hunting is the primary domain
Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-10-19

## Context
Ripple needs a live domain where facts change often, results are checkable, and the
author can reach real users.

## Decision
Internship and job hunting is the full product and pilot. News is benchmark-only.
Finance is an unseen-domain test only.

## Alternatives considered
- General personal assistant — too broad to label and evaluate.
- Finance first — overlaps the author's earlier ARGUS project; advice risk.
- News first — no natural personal pilot.

## Consequences
Pilot timing depends on the placement calendar.

## Revisit if
Fewer than 10 pilot users are recruited by 2027-03-31.
